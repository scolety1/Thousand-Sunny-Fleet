# Legacy Compatibility and History

Historical evidence and receipts are immutable facts.

- No prior result, receipt, review, soak, or blocker file is rewritten or deleted.
- Legacy freeform responses remain readable and preserved under `LEGACY_FAIL_CLOSED_V1`.
- Legacy structured V1 claims remain readable under `LEGACY_STRUCTURED_CLAIM_V1_FAIL_CLOSED`.
- Old generic text cannot gain admission through new verification or replay.
- Previously admitted records remain historical records produced under their then-current contract version.
- Recovery and audit surfaces may describe the superseded contract boundary without relabeling the historical record.

The failed semantic-calibration Tester verdict and architectural blocker evidence remain preserved under their original hashes.
